## 1. Introduction
At **BS.Framework**, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy. **MediaFlow Studio** is a locally hosted desktop media management workstation designed for photographers, videographers, and creative studios.

This Privacy Policy explains how our desktop application handles user data, file permissions, and system resources in compliance with the Microsoft Store Developer Agreement, GDPR, CCPA, and global data privacy regulations.

---

## 2. Zero Personal Data Collection
MediaFlow Studio operates entirely as an **offline-first, client-side application**:
- **No Personal Information Collected:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data (such as your name, email address, IP address, payment details, or geolocation).
- **No Background Telemetry or Tracking:** MediaFlow Studio contains zero analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, background telemetry, or third-party cookies.
- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to register, sign in, or provide credentials to use the application.

---

## 3. Local File System & Media Access
MediaFlow Studio requires access to your local drives, external SSDs, SD cards, and chosen storage destinations strictly to perform user-initiated operations:
- **Media Ingestion & Sorting:** Reading source media, renaming files, creating designated directory structures, and copying files.
- **Metadata Processing:** Reading EXIF timestamps and camera metadata locally to calculate time-shifts and categorize files.
- **Report Generation:** Writing local audit logs, separation reports, and Excel spreadsheets to your specified destination folder.

**At no point are your images, videos, spreadsheets, or folder paths uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud service.** All processing occurs 100% locally on your computer's CPU and memory.

---

## 4. Hardware and System Information
The application may inspect local system metrics (such as available CPU cores, RAM availability, and disk read/write throughput) strictly in real-time to optimize multi-threaded file copying and display live performance metrics on your dashboard. This information is volatile, processed in memory, and never logged or transmitted externally.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
MediaFlow Studio does not integrate with any third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or cloud storage providers by default. The software does not share any data with third parties.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Because MediaFlow Studio does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect or solicit data from children under the age of 13.

---

## 7. Security of Your Data
Since all files remain strictly on your physical storage devices, the security of your media files is governed by your local operating system security and access controls.

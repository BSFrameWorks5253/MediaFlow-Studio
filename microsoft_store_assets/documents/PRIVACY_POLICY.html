Privacy Policy

DataFlow Studio

Product: DataFlow Studio v4.0 • Publisher: BSFrameWorks • Effective Date: October 11, 2026
Key Principle: DataFlow Studio is engineered on a strict 100% offline, local-first architecture. Your media files, photographs, videos, and production reports remain entirely on your computer hardware. We do not collect, transmit, profile, or sell your personal data.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Media Collection: All media operations (renaming, sorting, categorization, Excel fusion) occur locally across your machine's physical storage drives. No files are ever sent to external servers.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: The application does not contain analytics beacons, usage tracking libraries, or advertising SDKs.
  • No Identity Harvesting: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide personal credentials to use the application.

2. Local Preferences & Storage

DataFlow Studio retains minimal, non-identifiable technical settings stored locally within your device's standard local application directory (%APPDATA%\DataFlow Studio\):

  • Visual preferences (UI dark theme selection, zoom factor).
  • Recent local folder paths chosen for workflow operations.
  • Local manifest generation settings.

3. Optional Cloud Integration

If you optionally choose to link Google Drive, authentication is established securely via official OAuth 2.0 endpoints. Tokens are stored locally on your device, and access is restricted solely to the directories you deliberately manage.

4. Data Security & Integrity

The application employs transactional atomic operations to prevent file corruption during large batch moves and features pre-flight storage probes to protect against drive overflow crashes.

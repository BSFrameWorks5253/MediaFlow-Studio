# 🛡️ Privacy Policy — DataFlow Studio

**Last Updated:** October 11, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026  
**Publisher:** BSFrameWorks  
**Product:** DataFlow Studio (Version 4.0 and higher)  

---

## 1. Overview
BSFrameWorks is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices for **DataFlow Studio**, a professional desktop application designed for media ingestion, synchronization, and report fusion.

**Key Commitment:** DataFlow Studio operates on a **100% local, offline-first principle**. We do not collect, harvest, sell, or transmit your personal data, media files, or file metadata to any external servers.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
* **No Media File Collection**: DataFlow Studio processes your media files (photos, videos, audio, manifests) strictly within your local computer's storage and attached drives. No files are ever uploaded to BSFrameWorks servers.
* **No Telemetry or Tracking**: The application does not contain behavioral telemetry, user activity trackers, usage profiling, or third-party advertising SDKs.
* **No Personal Identity Harvesting**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or IP addresses during your usage of the application.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage & Configuration
DataFlow Studio stores minimal, non-identifying operational preferences locally on your Windows device (in `%APPDATA%\DataFlow Studio\` or local application storage):
* **User Interface Preferences**: Selected color theme (e.g. Obsidian Pro, Sunset Glow), zoom scale factor.
* **Workflow Configuration**: Local directory paths previously selected for media source and destination folders.
* **Local Audit Manifests**: Excel and text audit reports generated at your explicit direction, which are saved exclusively to the local directories you designate.

All local configuration files can be reviewed, edited, or deleted by you at any time.

---

## 4. Optional Third-Party Cloud Integrations
DataFlow Studio provides optional integration capabilities for users who choose to connect external storage services (such as Google Drive):
* **Authentication**: If you connect Google Drive, authentication is handled via official OAuth 2.0 flows. Your authentication tokens are stored locally on your device in secure credential storage.
* **Scope of Access**: The application only interacts with Google Drive files and folders that you explicitly choose to upload, synchronize, or view.
* **Zero Intermediary Access**: BSFrameWorks never proxies, intercepts, or retains your third-party cloud credentials or files.

---

## 5. Security & Device Safety
* **Atomic File Operations**: DataFlow Studio employs transactional file rename and copy protocols to protect your media files from data corruption during moves across physical drives.
* **Disk Capacity Verification**: Built-in pre-flight storage probes ensure your target drive has sufficient free capacity before beginning large transfers, preventing system crashes caused by full storage.
* **Windows Security Compliance**: The application runs within standard Windows security boundaries and does not require unauthorized kernel or administrative privileges for normal operation.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
DataFlow Studio is a productivity utility for professional media workflows and does not knowingly collect any data from children. It is rated for all ages (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone).
